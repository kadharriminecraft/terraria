MOBILE: The left joystick (bottom-left) moves you — hold it down to drop through platforms. The right joystick (amber) aims and automatically uses your item: point it and hold to swing swords, fire guns, cast spells — and with a pickaxe or axe held it becomes a SMART CURSOR just like Terraria: point straight down to dig the two blocks under your feet, straight left/right for a walk-height 3-block tunnel, and diagonals carve a staircase. JUMP jumps — hold it in water to swim up (you can hop right out onto the bank). USE fires your held item. Tap & hold anywhere else on the screen to mine blocks or place them (yes, even on the left half!). Tap chests and NPCs to interact. Long-press any item for details.

MAP: The minimap (top right) follows you and fills in as you explore — tap it to open the full world map. On the big map you can pinch to zoom (or scroll on desktop), drag to pan, and use the + / − / FIT buttons. Gold dot = you, green = spawn. A huge world with big palm beaches at both far edges, sky islands floating high above the surface, and the Underworld burning at the bottom.

PLATFORMS: Wood platforms (craft: 1 wood → 2) let you walk right over them — hold DOWN (joystick down / S key) to drop through. You will find them as floors in the Underworld ruins and the Dungeon. Water rests on top of them, just like Terraria.

BOSSES: The Eater of Worlds is a giant segmented worm — cut it in half and both halves keep fighting (kill every segment to win; the head bites hardest). The Underworld ruins have chests inside their walls - loot them for hellstone bars.

SOUND: Every action has a built-in sound — footsteps, digging, the drill, placing, splashes, jumps, combat, bosses. To use your OWN audio files, see the SOUND BOARD (pause menu): drop files in a public GitHub repo sounds/ folder and any file that loads overrides the built-in sound.

ENDGAME GEAR: The Molten Drill (14 hellstone bars + 6 diamonds + 8 iron bars, at an Anvil) drills through any block near-instantly. The ZENITH — forge the Fiery Greatsword, Muramasa, Shadowfang, Cthulhu Fang and Bone Sword into one blade that throws a storm of spinning swords.

DESKTOP: A/D or arrows move, Space/W jump, S drops through platforms. Mouse aims; click mines/places/attacks. 1-0 selects hotbar, E opens inventory, Esc pauses, F toggles flight, G toggles god mode.

ADMIN: Settings → ADMIN / CHEATS: flight, god mode, teleport to any biome, spawn bosses, set time, and give yourself any item in any quantity.

PROGRESSION: Chop trees → Workbench → mine Iron → Anvil & iron gear → dig for Diamonds → Life Crystals in caves → summon King Slime / Eye of Cthulhu → craft Worm Food for the Eater of Worlds → Queen Bee in the jungle → Cursed Skull for Skeletron at the Dungeon → mine Hellstone (needs Diamond pickaxe!) → forge Hellstone armor.

TIP: Torches = gel + wood. Stars fall at night → 3 make a Mana Crystal. Honey heals. Do not touch lava. INVENTORY has a SORT button; chests have QUICK STACK.
